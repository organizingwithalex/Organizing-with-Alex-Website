Smart storage for every kind of West Palm home

West Palm Beach has every kind of home: 1920s houses in El Cid and Flamingo Park with charming but small closets, new townhomes, and downtown condos where every inch counts. We design storage around the space you have, using vertical shelving, clear bins and labels so small closets and pantries work like big ones.

Pantries and kitchens are some of our most popular West Palm projects. We edit expired items, zone by how you cook and snack, and use airtight containers that stand up to Florida humidity. Closets get the same treatment: edited, grouped by category and color, and set up with matching hangers.

We also help with moves in and out of West Palm Beach, including condo downsizing and unpacking new construction. We're based nearby in Palm Beach Gardens and serve Palm Beach, Lake Worth Beach and Wellington too.

West Palm Beach questions

Can you make a small condo closet work?

Yes. Small closets are where good systems matter most. We use slim hangers, double rods, shelf dividers and over-door storage to fit more without the clutter.

Do you help with downsizing?

Yes. We help you decide what to keep, sell, donate or pass on, then set up the new home so it feels calm from day one.

How much does a professional organizer cost in West Palm Beach?

Hands-on organizing starts at $100 per hour for one organizer and $130 per hour for two. Most projects are custom quoted after a free consultation, and recurring plans start at $600 per month.

Do I need to be home during the project?

No. Many clients give us a key or door code. We send photo updates as we go, and everything we see stays private.
