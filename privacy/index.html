Privacy policy

Who we are

Organizing with Alex is a professional home organizing business based in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida, serving Palm Beach County, Broward and Miami-Dade. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Organizing with Alex. You can reach us at organizingwithalex@gmail.com or (516) 639-7928.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a form, book a call, text, call or email us, we collect what you share: usually your name, phone number, email, the area you live in and details about your home and project.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our site and its service providers may collect basic technical information, such as your browser type, device, pages viewed and general location, through cookies and similar tools. We use this to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations and projects, prepare quotes, provide our services, send appointment reminders and follow-ups, and improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

We use trusted third-party services to run our business, including Formspree (to deliver website form submissions to our email), Google (for email, calendar booking and website analytics) and our website hosting provider. These providers process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Photos of your home

We may photograph spaces before and after a project for our records. We only share photos of your home on our website or social media with your permission, and we never share your name or address with them.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and client information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet our business, tax and legal obligations, then delete or securely store it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing organizingwithalex@gmail.com. You can also set your browser to refuse cookies, although some parts of the site may not work as intended.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
