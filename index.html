Questions

How much does professional organizing cost?

Every home is different, so projects are quoted after a free consultation. As a guide, organizing starts at $100 per hour for one organizer, and recurring plans start at $600 a month. You'll get a written quote before any work begins.

Do I have to be home?

No. Many clients hand us the keys and come home to a finished space.

Will you make me throw things away?

Never. You decide what stays; we make it easy to let go of the rest.

What happens to the things I don't keep?

We take care of it. Items that deserve a second life are dropped off at local donation partners, so they go to someone who will use and love them. No trips, no piles left in your garage.

How long does a project take?

A single pantry or closet usually takes one day. Whole-home projects and move-ins are planned over several days, and we'll map out the schedule with you before we start.

Do you bring the organizing products?

Yes. We source, deliver and install bins, containers and labels chosen for your space, or work with what you already own. Products are billed separately at cost.

Do you work with seasonal residents?

Yes. Our Snowbird Care plan covers your arrival, a mid-season refresh and closing the house, coordinated with your home-watch company or house manager.

How do I keep it organized afterward?

Every project ends with personalized upkeep videos, strategies and folding guides for you or your housekeeper. Many clients also add a Monthly Refresh or Seasonal Reset.

Do you still serve Miami?

Yes. We serve Miami-Dade and Broward, including Miami Beach, Key Biscayne, Coral Gables and Fort Lauderdale, with every project planned by Alex.

Are you insured?

Yes. Our organizers are background-checked, and we carry liability insurance. We're happy to send a certificate of insurance to your building or HOA on request.
